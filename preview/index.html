08 · Motion

Soft ease-out reveals, slow ambient drift.

Everything on this page that slid in used cubic-bezier(.22,.61,.36,1) over 750ms with 100ms stagger. Blobs drift on 38–44s loops. Reduced-motion turns all of it off.

Reveal

750ms · ease-reveal

opacity 0 → 1, translateY(24px) → 0, delays .1/.2/.3s. Scroll back up and down to replay.

Hover

220ms · lift + glow

Buttons: translateY(-1px) scale(1.018). Cards: translateY(-5px) in 300ms. Arrows nudge 2px in 150ms.

Menus

150–500ms · ease-out

Mega-menu scales from the top in 150ms; the header's glass ring fades in over 500ms once you scroll.
